Introduction

Baby Names respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Baby Names mobile application (also referred to as Baby Names Together) and related services.

Baby Names helps parents and partners discover baby names by browsing and swiping through a name catalog, saving likes and dislikes, filtering by preferences, linking with a partner to find shared matches, hearing pronunciations, and using an optional AI baby-name assistant.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name or display name, email address, authentication details, and profile settings associated with your account (including Google Sign-In where used).
  • Name preferences: filters and settings you choose such as gender preference, origin, style, letter rules, name length, and related discovery preferences.
  • Likes and dislikes: baby names you like, dislike, undo, or otherwise mark while browsing or reviewing lists.
  • Partner connection data: partner invite codes, partner-link status, and shared match information when you choose to connect with a partner.
  • Onboarding information: details you provide during setup (such as your name and initial preferences) to personalize the experience.
  • AI chat content: messages and prompts you send to the Baby Name AI assistant, and responses generated for you during that session.
  • Purchase information: product identifiers and purchase/restore status for optional premium (PRO) features processed through the app store billing system. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Device and usage information: app preferences, language/theme settings, crash or diagnostic data (where enabled), network connectivity status, and technical information needed to operate and improve the app.

Permissions Used

Baby Names may request device permissions only as needed to provide features you use:

  • Internet Connection: account authentication, cloud sync, name catalog access, partner matching, AI processing, billing (where applicable), and service communication.
  • Camera / Photos / Media: selecting or capturing images where a feature requires it (for example profile or sharing-related actions).
  • Storage / Files: saving or accessing files needed for app features such as sharing or local storage on your device.
  • Microphone: optional voice-related features where enabled (for example voice input). Audio is used only to provide the feature you request.
  • Notifications: optional push or in-app notifications for updates and service messages when enabled by you.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work without the related permission.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide, operate, and improve Baby Names services, including to:

  • Create and manage your account and profile
  • Show baby-name cards and catalog results based on your filters and preferences
  • Save, sync, and display your likes, dislikes, and favorites
  • Connect you with a partner via invite codes and show names you both like
  • Provide name details, meanings, origins, and pronunciation (including on-device text-to-speech)
  • Power the Baby Name AI assistant for name ideas and related questions
  • Process optional premium purchases and restore purchases through the app store billing system
  • Support sharing and invite features where available
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve app reliability and performance

AI Processing

When you use the Baby Name AI assistant, the messages and preferences you provide may be processed to generate name suggestions, meanings, origins, style ideas, and related baby-name guidance. The assistant is intended for baby-name topics only.

AI outputs are generated based on your inputs and may be imperfect. You remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated suggestions before relying on them. Chat history for a session is used to continue the conversation and is not intended as a permanent public record.

Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard security practices to help protect your personal information. Your information is processed only for providing Baby Names services.

Depending on the feature, data may be stored locally on your device and/or synced to secure cloud services associated with your account (for example authentication, profile, likes/dislikes, and partner connections). Access controls, encryption in transit, and platform security measures are used where applicable.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We continuously work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third Party Services

Baby Names may use trusted third-party services to operate the app. These may include:

  • Firebase / Google services: authentication, cloud database (Firestore), messaging, analytics/crash reporting, and related infrastructure where applicable.
  • Google Sign-In / Google Play Services / App Store billing: sign-in, notifications, and in-app purchases or premium unlocks where applicable.
  • AI processing providers: third-party AI services (such as Google Gemini) used to generate baby-name assistance when you use that feature.
  • Device and platform services: text-to-speech, sharing, network status, and other operating-system capabilities required for core features.

These providers process information only as needed to deliver the requested services and according to their own privacy practices and applicable agreements.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information associated with your account
  • Update or correct inaccurate information in the app (such as your display name and preferences)
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw optional permissions or disable notifications through device settings
  • Disconnect a partner link or clear likes/dislikes where the app provides those controls

You may request deletion or correction of your personal information by contacting us or using the Delete Account page. We may need to verify your request before completing it.
