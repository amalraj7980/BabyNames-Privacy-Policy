Introduction

You can request deletion of your Baby Names account and associated personal data at any time.

This page explains how to submit a deletion request, what data will be removed, and how long retention may apply after your request is verified.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Baby Names account and associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to careermateai.support@gmail.com.
  2. Include the email address registered with your Baby Names account.
  3. Clearly state that you are requesting account deletion.
  4. Our support team will verify your identity and process the request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following data associated with your account will be deleted:

  • Account information and email address
  • Display name and profile settings
  • Name preferences and filters
  • Liked and disliked baby names
  • Partner connection / invite-link data
  • Shared match records tied to your account
  • Onboarding and local preference data associated with your account
  • AI assistant session content linked to your account (where stored)

Data Retention

After we receive and verify your request, your account and associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days.

Some limited information may be retained where required for legal, security, accounting, or compliance purposes (for example app-store purchase records managed by Google Play or the App Store).

Contact Support

If you have questions about account deletion or need help with your request, contact us at:

Email: careermateai.support@gmail.com
